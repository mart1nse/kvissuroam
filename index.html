Free 3D driving game in Tartu, Estonia

Drive and race through Tartu, rebuilt in 3D from real map, lidar and aerial photo data. Time-attack the Toomemägi street circuit and the Majoraadi gravel rally stage, roam the city freely and race other players online. Free in the browser on a computer or phone, nothing to install.
